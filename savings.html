Annual Percentage Yield (APY) is variable and may change at any time, including after your account is opened. No minimum balance is required to open the account or to obtain the advertised APY. Fees may reduce earnings.

How the interest works

The rate applies to the entire balance rather than to a tier of it, so there is no threshold to reach and no portion of the balance earning a lower rate. Interest is credited to the account, and the Interest Details screen in online banking shows the yield, what has accrued and the payment history.

Certificates of Deposit are offered subject to the terms disclosed in the applicable account agreement at the time an account is opened.
