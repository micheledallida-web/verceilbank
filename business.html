Talk to us about business banking

Accounts for businesses

Verceil Bank offers business checking accounts, business savings accounts and cash management accounts. The features, fees, interest rates, transaction limits and eligibility that apply to each are disclosed in the applicable account agreement.

Accounts may be opened by eligible businesses subject to identity verification, Know Your Customer requirements, sanctions and compliance screening, risk assessment and approval by the Bank — the same conditions set out in clause 3 of the Customer Banking Policy.

Opening a business account

A business account needs documentation beyond a personal one, and what is needed depends on how the business is constituted. Get in touch and we will tell you what to bring before you start rather than after.
