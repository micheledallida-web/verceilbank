Home loans & home equity

Speak to us about home lending

Home Equity Line of Credit

A Verceil Bank HELOC turns your home equity into flexible purchasing power for renovations, consolidation, or major expenses, with competitive variable rates and convenient access to funds right when you need them.

Subject to credit approval and property valuation. A home equity line of credit is secured against your home. Rates are variable, and the rate, fees and repayment terms that apply to your line are disclosed in the credit agreement before you accept it.

Talk it through first

Home lending is the product where the numbers depend most on your circumstances. Speak to us before you apply and we will go through what you can borrow, what it would cost and what the line would be secured against.
